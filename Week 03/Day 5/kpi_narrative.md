# Week 3 Day 5 - KPI Narrative

## KPI Dashboard Summary

The KPI dashboard provides a single-page view of the retail sales performance using revenue, sales volume, customer activity, discounts, returns, and channel performance.

## Key Trends

Total Revenue and Units Sold provide an overview of overall business performance. Average Order Value and Revenue per Unit help monitor the value generated from each transaction and unit sold.

The Website Revenue Share KPI measures the contribution of the online channel to total revenue. Customer Purchase Frequency indicates how frequently customers are generating transactions.

## Anomalies and Areas to Monitor

The Return Rate is an important operational KPI because a higher return rate can reduce realized revenue and indicate potential product or customer-experience issues.

The Discount Rate should also be monitored because excessive discounting can increase sales volume while reducing revenue per transaction.

Category Revenue and Channel Revenue charts help identify differences in performance across product categories and sales channels.

## KPI Classification

Leading indicators include Discount Rate, Website Revenue Share, and Customer Purchase Frequency because they can provide signals about future sales performance.

Lagging indicators include Total Revenue, Units Sold, Average Order Value, Return Rate, Revenue per Unit, Category Revenue, and Channel Revenue because they measure outcomes that have already occurred.

## Management Focus

The dashboard should be reviewed regularly to identify changes in revenue, customer activity, returns, discounts, and channel contribution. Significant changes in these KPIs should be investigated together rather than viewed independently, since changes in discounts, returns, and channel mix can affect overall revenue performance.

# W11D5 - Stakeholder Report: 5 Business Insights

## Project Overview

Analyzed sales data and prepared a stakeholder report with five business insights, charts, and recommendations.

## Business Objective

Understand sales performance across regions, product categories, and months to support business decisions.

## Dataset

- Source: Week 07/Day 3/Sales_Data.csv
- Period: January to September 2026
- Transactions: 24
- Regions: North, South, East, West
- Categories: Electronics, Grocery, Clothing, Furniture

## Tools Used

- Python 3.11
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- python-docx
- VS Code
- Git and GitHub

## Key Metrics

| Metric                    |      Result |
| ------------------------- | ----------: |
| Total Revenue             | Rs. 308,400 |
| Average Transaction Value |  Rs. 12,850 |
| Total Units Sold          |       1,079 |
| Transactions              |          24 |

## Five Business Insights

1. South generated the highest regional revenue at Rs. 89,200.
2. Electronics generated the highest category revenue at Rs. 133,000.
3. June recorded the highest monthly sales at Rs. 43,000.
4. Grocery sold 542 units but generated Rs. 35,300 in revenue.
5. Furniture recorded the highest revenue per unit at approximately Rs. 1,235.71. This is not profit per unit.

## Recommendations

- Study South's successful sales practices.
- Monitor Electronics demand and inventory.
- Investigate the factors behind June's higher sales.
- Review Grocery pricing and product mix.
- Evaluate Furniture costs and margins.

## Limitations

- The dataset contains only 24 transactions.
- Product costs and profit margins are unavailable.
- The analysis identifies patterns but does not establish their causes.
- Additional data is required to validate the findings.

# Sales Performance – Data Storytelling Report

## 1. Business Context

A retail company wants to understand its sales performance across different regions and product categories. The management team needs clear insights to identify strong-performing areas and opportunities for improvement.

## 2. Business Objective

The objective is to analyze sales data and communicate meaningful findings through visualizations and a structured business narrative.

## 3. Dataset Overview

- Total transactions: 24
- Date range: January–September 2026
- Total sales: ₹3,08,400
- Total units sold: 1,079
- Average transaction value: ₹12,850
- Missing values: None

## 4. Key Findings

### Regional Performance

South generated the highest sales of ₹89,200, followed by West at ₹80,300 and North at ₹79,100. East recorded the lowest sales of ₹59,800.

**Insight:** South is the strongest region, while East needs further performance investigation.

### Category Performance

Electronics generated the highest revenue of ₹1,33,000. Furniture contributed ₹86,500, Clothing ₹53,600 and Grocery ₹35,300.

Grocery recorded the highest quantity sold, with 542 units, despite generating the lowest revenue.

**Insight:** Electronics is the leading revenue category, while Grocery has high sales volume but lower revenue.

### Monthly Sales Performance

June recorded the highest monthly sales of ₹43,000. January recorded the lowest monthly sales of ₹23,500.

Sales declined from ₹43,000 in June to ₹24,900 in September.

**Insight:** The business experienced weaker sales after June, indicating a trend that deserves further investigation.

## 5. Business Recommendations

1. Study the sales strategies used in the South region and identify practices that could help improve other regions.
2. Investigate the reasons behind lower sales in the East region.
3. Maintain product availability and marketing support for Electronics, the highest-revenue category.
4. Review Grocery pricing, product mix and promotional strategies to understand its high-volume, low-revenue performance.
5. Investigate the decline in sales after June using additional data such as customer demand, discounts and marketing activity.

## 6. Conclusion

The analysis shows that South is the leading region and Electronics is the highest-revenue category. However, regional differences and the decline in monthly sales after June highlight opportunities for improvement.

By using data-driven decisions, the company can improve regional performance, optimize product strategies and strengthen overall sales.

## 7. Limitations

- The analysis contains only 24 transactions.
- The dataset covers nine months.
- It does not include customer details, costs, discounts or marketing information.
- The findings describe observed patterns and do not establish the causes of sales changes.

Further analysis with more data is recommended before making major business decisions.

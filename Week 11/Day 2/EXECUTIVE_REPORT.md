# W11D2 – Executive Sales Report

## 1. Executive Overview
The analysis covers 24 sales transactions with total revenue of INR 308,400 and 1,079 units sold. The purpose is to identify business performance patterns and communicate findings through interactive visualizations and a PowerPoint report.

## 2. Regional Performance
South was the leading region, generating INR 89,200. East recorded the lowest revenue at INR 59,800.

Business implication: Study the factors contributing to South's performance and identify opportunities to improve East's sales.

## 3. Category Performance
Electronics generated the highest revenue at INR 133,000. Grocery generated the lowest revenue at INR 35,300, despite having the highest units sold.

Business implication: Review category pricing, product mix, and sales strategies to understand differences between revenue and units sold.

## 4. Monthly Performance
June recorded the highest monthly revenue at INR 43,000, while January recorded the lowest at INR 23,500. Sales declined to INR 24,900 by September.

Business implication: Investigate monthly fluctuations and evaluate whether seasonal demand or business activities explain the changes.

## 5. Recommendations
1. Analyse successful regional sales practices.
2. Develop improvement strategies for lower-performing regions.
3. Review product mix and revenue contribution by category.
4. Investigate the decline after June.
5. Monitor monthly revenue and sales volume through regular dashboards.

## 6. Limitations
The dataset has only 24 transactions covering nine months. Customer-level, cost, discount, and marketing information is unavailable. Therefore, findings describe observed patterns and do not establish causal relationships.

## 7. Conclusion
Combining Pandas, Plotly, and python-pptx creates a repeatable executive reporting workflow. Interactive charts and automated presentations help communicate findings clearly and support informed business decisions.

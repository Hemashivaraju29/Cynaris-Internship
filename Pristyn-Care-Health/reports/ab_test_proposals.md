# Pristyn Care Health – A/B Testing Proposals

## 1. Appointment Reminder Test

**Problem:** The overall appointment no-show rate is 16.20%.

**Hypothesis:** Sending an additional appointment reminder through SMS or WhatsApp one day before the appointment will reduce missed appointments.

**Control Group (A):** Patients receive the existing appointment reminders.

**Test Group (B):** Patients receive the existing reminders plus an additional reminder one day before the appointment.

**Success Metric:** Appointment no-show rate.

**Measurement:** Compare the no-show rates of the control and test groups.

## 2. General Medicine Confirmation Test

**Problem:** General Medicine has the highest specialty-wise no-show rate at 22.50%.

**Hypothesis:** Sending an additional appointment confirmation message will improve appointment attendance.

**Control Group (A):** Existing booking confirmation process.

**Test Group (B):** Existing booking confirmation plus an additional follow-up confirmation message.

**Success Metric:** General Medicine appointment attendance rate.

**Measurement:** Compare attendance rates between both groups.

## 3. Billing Clarity Test

**Problem:** The average billing clarity rating is 2.93 out of 5.

**Hypothesis:** Providing a simple, itemised billing explanation before payment will improve patient understanding and billing satisfaction.

**Control Group (A):** Existing billing communication.

**Test Group (B):** Provide an itemised bill explanation with clear service charges.

**Success Metric:** Average billing clarity rating.

**Measurement:** Compare the average billing clarity ratings collected from both groups after the billing process.

## 4. Patient Experience Follow-up Test

**Problem:** Sunrise East has the lowest average overall satisfaction score at 2.81 out of 5 in the available satisfaction dataset.

**Hypothesis:** A structured patient follow-up process will help identify and address patient concerns, improving overall satisfaction.

**Control Group (A):** Existing patient follow-up process.

**Test Group (B):** Send a structured feedback follow-up message and route reported concerns to the appropriate team.

**Success Metric:** Average overall satisfaction score.

**Measurement:** Compare satisfaction ratings between both groups after the follow-up process.

## Measurement and Evaluation

- Randomly assign eligible participants to control and test groups.
- Decide the sample size and test duration before starting each experiment.
- Keep the other conditions similar between the two groups.
- Measure the selected success metric for both groups.
- Check statistical significance before recommending full implementation.
- Monitor patient complaints and other relevant operational measures to ensure the changes do not create new problems.
- Protect patient information and follow applicable privacy requirements.

## Important Note

These are proposed experiments and have not yet been conducted. Their effectiveness cannot be confirmed until the tests are implemented and results are analysed.

The patient satisfaction findings used in Proposal 4 come from the Sunrise dataset. They should not be treated as confirmed Pristyn Care findings unless the dataset's organisation is verified.

HYPOTHESIS TESTING RESULTS
==========================
Test: Independent two-sample t-test
Comparison: TCS vs Reliance daily returns

H0: Mean daily returns of TCS and Reliance are equal.
H1: Mean daily returns of TCS and Reliance are different.

TCS mean daily return: 0.0873%
Reliance mean daily return: 0.0937%
T-statistic: -0.1321
P-value: 0.894918
Significance level: 0.05

Conclusion: Fail to reject H0: There is not enough evidence of a statistically significant difference.
